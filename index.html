Raising a Baller

Raise the athlete.Protect the person.

Youth sports can build confidence, discipline, resilience, and lifelong memories. They can also bring pressure, comparison, injuries, exhaustion, and difficult decisions. Raising a Baller grew from our family's experience navigating those realities while trying to protect the child behind the athlete.

Why we wrote it

We wrote Raising a Baller because youth sports ask families to make important decisions long before they feel fully prepared to make them. Parents are expected to navigate pressure, injuries, competition, coaching, confidence, identity, opportunity, and the growing business around youth sports, often while trying to protect the child behind the athlete.

This book grew from our own family's experience and the questions we kept asking along the way. How do we help athletes handle setbacks, pressure, and change? How do we keep the bigger picture in view when the next game can feel like everything?

Meet Team Schultz

Beyond the scoreboard

Sports shape more than performance

Raising a Baller is for parents who want to help their athletes compete, grow, and pursue opportunities while becoming grounded, resilient people beyond the game.

Wins, losses, rankings, statistics, and recruiting decisions can feel urgent in the moment. Their deeper influence often appears later, in how athletes respond to pressure, recover from disappointment, communicate, manage expectations, and understand who they are. That is why the conversation cannot focus only on performance.

Whole-athlete development

Support physical growth, emotional well-being, identity, confidence, character, and a healthy relationship with competition.

Better parent decisions

Navigate pressure, expectations, injuries, opportunities, technology, and changing family dynamics.

Lessons that last

Help athletes carry resilience, leadership, accountability, and self-awareness beyond sports.
