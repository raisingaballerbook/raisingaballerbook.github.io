Raising a Baller

Raising an athlete in today's world

Youth sports can build confidence, discipline, leadership, and lifelong memories. They can also bring pressure, comparison, exhaustion, and difficult choices. Raising a Baller helps parents navigate both sides while keeping their athlete's character, well-being, and sense of self at the center.

About the book

Raising a Baller is for parents who want to raise more than a successful athlete. The book explores the realities families face behind the highlights, including pressure, comparison, injuries, recovery, identity, family decisions, technology, opportunity, and the growing business of youth sports.

Through real stories, practical guidance, and honest reflection, it helps parents make more intentional decisions while keeping their athlete's character, well-being, and sense of self at the center. The goal is not to create a perfect athlete or provide one formula for every family. It is to help parents raise grounded, resilient young people who understand that their worth extends far beyond the scoreboard.

"The scoreboard fades. Character lasts."

What readers will find

Guidance for the athlete, the parent, and the life beyond the game

The book looks beyond wins, losses, and recruiting outcomes to help families understand the athlete as a whole person. Each section offers practical insight for navigating development, pressure, identity, family decisions, and the lessons that remain long after the final game.

The whole athlete

Physical development, emotional well-being, confidence, personal identity, and character.

The parent's role

Pressure, expectations, difficult conversations, injuries, opportunities, and family decisions.

Life beyond the game

Resilience, accountability, leadership, self-awareness, and the lessons that last.
